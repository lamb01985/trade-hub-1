## Isolated patch

This branch starts at the exact serving production commit. It is a worktree of the canonical repository, not another application. The original black/neon-green Plan → Trade → Review shell, original tools, setup-rule implementation, journal and chart remain.

Plan opens Levels by default. A single verified current-session subscription supplies header, map, chart and alerts; URLs preserve symbol/workspace/view through Live Map, ticker changes, reload and Back. Original archival research is accessible only under Review → Historical Level Maps. Archived values never enter the current subscription. Inactive panels no longer mount their market consumers merely because CSS hides them.

The server-side normalized desk adapter reuses production's credential-contained Massive/Polygon transport. Previous high/low/close are from the previous completed regular session; session range, premarket, VWAP and SMA-seeded EMA9/20/50 derive from validated completed current-date minutes. Price-ranked references use actual supplied prices; next scenario targets must have a distinct price beyond the boundary. Confluence clusters have a full span no greater than 0.05% of the previous close, with actual constituent bounds. These are descriptive calculations, not proof of a trading edge.

Observations poll at 30 seconds and context at 60 seconds, or five minutes while closed, serially per resource. Provider Retry-After is preserved without immediate transport retry; cooldown blocks the map refresh button. Failed requests clear their data. Last-successful refresh time is retained separately without retaining stale calculations. Dates, symbols, source times and feed quality gate scenarios and alerts. Level alert memory is symbol-scoped. Legacy setup and coach announcements cannot act on timestamp-unverified bundles; their underlying rule engines are preserved. No new broker execution or order staging is enabled.

One authenticated /api/desk function also handles session/login/logout to stay within the existing project's function allowance. Native ESM cold starts, private/no-store responses, Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite cookies, origin checking, persistent hashed sessions and logout are tested. No credentials or project settings are changed.
